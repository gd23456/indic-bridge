# IndicBridge demo: the tunnel, then the proof

Scope: the single local web app served by demo/server.py. Visitor mode: hero and story sections are Read, the bench is Operate. Audience: faculty at a viva; a presenter drives it on the M4 MacBook, often projected, Wi-Fi off. Job: make the experiment visible and checkable live. Constraints: every number from results/ or data/, "not run yet" shown honestly, fully offline (bundled fonts), light default with a dark theme. User request (4 Nov): far less text, minimalist, Monaspace fonts, the perspective-tunnel hero as the background, keep the orb, nothing like the Neralu hackathon site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The test set is the stage. Real FLEURS sentences in Devanagari, Kannada and Odia fly out of a 3D tunnel toward the viewer, and the orb sits at the vanishing point turning speech into one more card. Refuses the starfield-and-neon of the team's hackathon site and the record-button-over-a-textbox ASR default.

OWN-WORLD: Light paper #f4f4f2, white cards, 1px graphite lines, near-black ink, one electric indigo for identity, actions and the orb; red only for wrong words; Hindi gold and English blue only in data. Type is GitHub Next's Monaspace, self-hosted: Xenon slab, wide and heavy, for display; Neon for everything else; Radon handwriting for one phrase per headline and rare margin notes. Dot grid and two depth rings on the hero. Dark theme: #0a0a0c with a lighter indigo.

STORY: The panel reads one question, plays a clip and watches every model write it with errors marked, scrolls to see why Odia is hard (its sentence breaking into byte chips), reads the result in one chart, then the presenter uses the bench.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height paper stage, dot grid, depth rings centred on the orb at 46%. Behind everything about fourteen sentence cards (language, sentence in its script, duration) drift from deep z toward the viewer with pointer parallax; page scroll pushes them faster. Top bar: mark, IndicBridge ସେତୁ, three lowercase links, live network dot, theme toggle. Upper centre: H1 "Language, or just data?" in Xenon with "or just data?" in Radon, one Neon line under it. Bottom centre: the MorphOrb pill (language, level meter, test clip, indigo mic). On submit the tunnel slows and dims while the orb unfolds into the result card.

FORM: pinned by the user (tunnel background, MorphOrb, Monaspace, minimalist); no concept-seed roll; seed key: user-pinned.

SIGNATURE: A live transcript flies into the tunnel as a new indigo-edged card marked "you, just now", joining the test sentences; error ignition stays on every lane.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
